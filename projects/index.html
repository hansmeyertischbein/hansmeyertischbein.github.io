  1. 29 Oct 2026

    Finance

    Fund operations: from trade to reconciled holdings and NAV

    A simulation of a day in fund operations, with three fictional funds holding real Oslo Børs shares and Norwegian government bonds. Each trade is validated, enriched, checked against the mandate (issuer, sector and ESG limits), matched with the broker, settled on T+2 and reconciled with the custodian before it ends up in the fund’s NAV. Rule-based fixes happen automatically, the rest goes to an exception queue with manager approval. Move the clock through the day to see trades settle and NAV be released. In Norwegian.

    Trade lifecycle, reconciliation, NAV calculation, JavaScript, Python, Yahoo Finance and Norges Bank data

  3. 22 Oct 2026

    Finance

    Do not short yesterday’s winners

    A daily short strategy on OSEBX stocks: when a stock rises at least 5%, short it at the next open and buy it back at the close. With 0.1% per transaction it beat OSEBX over 2021–2025, but mainly because of 2021: in 2022–2025 it returned 26% against 39% for the index, and it turns negative at about 0.12% per transaction. Includes the same test over ten years.

    Backtesting, Python, JavaScript, Yahoo Finance data

  6. 16 Oct 2026

    Finance

    Forecasting risk on Oslo Børs with machine learning

    Can machine learning forecast next month’s volatility and large falls for Norwegian stocks better than the simple models the industry uses? A walk-forward test from 2015 to 2026: 13% lower volatility error than HAR, no gain from machine learning trained directly on falls, and a two-step model that does help. Includes what did not work and how it was handled, and live forecasts that are scored every month.

    Python, LightGBM, random forest, neural networks, HAR, Diebold–Mariano, SHAP, GitHub Actions

    See the results and forecasts Read the paper (PDF) Code on GitHub
