CV generator

Upload the CV you have, or start from scratch, and get a clean one-page version as a Word file or PDF. The tool reads your CV into the form, scales the text so it always fits on one page, and points out bullet points that need a number or a stronger verb. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

Start from your CV

Upload a PDF or Word file and the form fills itself in. The file is read in your browser and never sent anywhere. Check the result: layouts differ, so some lines may land in the wrong place.

Improve the wording with AI
