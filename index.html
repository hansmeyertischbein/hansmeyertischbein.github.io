Young adults and insurance loyalty

Group project in Methods and Insight at BI (spring 2026): a market study of what drives loyalty among 18–29-year-olds in Norway, using If P&C Insurance as the case. We ran three focus groups, surveyed 187 respondents and identified four customer segments through cluster analysis. Among those who had filed a claim, satisfaction with the claims process was the strongest driver of loyalty.

JMP, focus groups, cluster analysis, ANOVA, regression
