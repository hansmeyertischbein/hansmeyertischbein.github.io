Projects

What does it cost to wait?

A conceptual study of how to prioritise tasks. Starting from a grid of importance and urgency with time spent as a third axis, I develop the model in five steps and test each one against theory from scheduling, finance, decision analysis and behavioural economics. The result is an index that ranks tasks by cost of delay per calibrated hour, with urgency calculated from the slack to each deadline, and a feasibility check that shows overload days before it becomes a crisis.

Scheduling theory, decision analysis, behavioural economics

Young adults and insurance loyalty

Group project in Methods and Insight at BI (spring 2026): a market study of what drives loyalty among 18–29-year-olds in Norway, using If P&C Insurance as the case. We ran three focus groups, surveyed 187 respondents and identified four customer segments through cluster analysis. Among those who had filed a claim, satisfaction with the claims process was the strongest driver of loyalty.

JMP, focus groups, cluster analysis, ANOVA, regression
